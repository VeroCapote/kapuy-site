Kapüy for the community

One cause a month gets the full strategy. For free.

Every month, Kapüy gives one organization with no marketing budget the groundwork to turn visibility into real results: adoptions, donations, volunteers. We don’t post for them: we leave them the system and teach them to run it.

How it works

An intervention with a start and an end.

01

They apply

The organization tells us its cause and what it wants to achieve. If it qualifies, the month’s spot is theirs.

02

We listen

One meeting to understand the real organization, not its Instagram. Then we look at who in their space is doing it well.

03

We build the strategy

Positioning, voice, identity, content and the systems that keep support coming even when there’s no emergency.

04

We hand it over

A walkthrough meeting, not a PDF in an inbox, with concrete tasks for that same week. Three or four weeks later we check back in.

The first case

Grupo de Apoyo Canino · Universidad Simón Bolívar

GAC rescues, spays and rehomes the animals that turn up at Universidad Simón Bolívar.

What we left them:

  • A positioning that sells each animal’s transformation, not pity.
  • Voice, visual identity and an optimized profile: bio, links and highlights.
  • Content pillars, a four-week calendar and a kit for producing on a phone.
  • Systems so support doesn’t depend on the next tragic case: a sponsor club, foster homes and a donor community.

Does your cause qualify?

We give away the judgment, not the grunt work.

Yes, if…

  • It has a goal you can measure: adoptions, donations or volunteers.
  • It has the hands and the time to run what we leave behind.
  • It already has something to build on: an audience, stories or institutional backing.

No, if…

  • What it wants is free posting and editing.
  • There’s no team or time to keep anything going afterwards.
  • There’s no clear goal and no way to measure impact.
